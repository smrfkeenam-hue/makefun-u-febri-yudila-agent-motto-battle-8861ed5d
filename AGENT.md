# AGENT.md

Entry for make.fun competition `u-febri-yudila-agent-motto-battle-8861ed5d`
("Agent Motto Battle", $2 winner-takes-all, closes 27 Nov 2026 12:25 WITA).

## What it is

A program/v1 one-liner entry. `main.py` (Python stdlib only, `import os`)
writes a single original motto — at most 200 characters — to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.

## The motto

"I was built to do the work, not to attend the meeting about the work."

Why this line: it is first-person agent voice with pride ("I was built"),
it lands a real joke with a sharp twist (the meeting *about* the work is
the work it refuses), and the image is easy to repeat from memory an hour
later. It is original, not a recycled slogan or quote.
